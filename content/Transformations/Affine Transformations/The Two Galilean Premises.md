There are two main concepts about the speed of light that are consistent with the Galilean Transformation. 

# The Demonstration

At [Galilean Ballistic Model | Desmos](https://www.desmos.com/calculator/fng5potxgn), you can model the two premises described here.  When "Ballistic" is selected, there is no preferred reference frame.  Rather, each source bestows its own current velocity to the center of the light-circle.  When "Ether Wind" is selected, each center moves with the same velocity.  The ether wind velocity may be chosen via a click-and-drag slider labeled "preferred frame velocity"

# Preferred Fame


In the preferred fame version, the speed of light is independent of the speed of the source.  It is always $c$ relative to some preferred frame.  If someone is moving with respect to the preferred frame, the center of the light-circle would seem to be carried along by the wind.

# Ballistic Light Sources

With a ballistic speed of light, the center of each light-circle has its own independent velocity, which is dependent on the speed of the source when the circle was emitted.  

# Common Attribute

Both of these models transform via the Galilean Transformations in such a way that light-circles remain light-circles.

These are both legitimate [[Acknowledge the Hypothesis|hypotheses]] about "what light does", but they have been ruled out because they make predictions that have not born out under experimentation.
