The Galilean Transformation:
$$
t'=t
\\x'=x-vt
$$
or in matrix form

$$
\begin{pmatrix}t'\\x'\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-v&1\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}t\\x\end{pmatrix}
$$

See [[The Two Galilean Premises]] for two versions of physics associated with the Galilean Transformation.  See [[Aberration]] for a discussion of how this transformation ought be applied to distant starlight.
