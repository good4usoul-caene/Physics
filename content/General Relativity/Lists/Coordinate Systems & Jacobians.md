
Examples of Coordinate systems:

Stationary 
 - [[Polar Coordinates + Time]]  $(r,\phi,t)$  (Stationary coordinates in the same inertial frame as (x,y,t))
 - [[Schwarzschild]]  The Schwarzschild metric obtains a static relation between $r'$ and $r$, between $\phi'$ and $\phi$ and does not modify $t$.  
 Rotating
 - [[Head Turning Coordinates]] : $r,\phi',t$  (Coordinates that keep the time and distance as (x,y,t) but move "as the head turns" of a central observer.)
 - [[Modified Head Turning Coordinates]]: $(r,\phi'/\gamma, \tau)$  (Coordinates that move "as the head turns" of a central observer, but captures the Lorentz Contraction and Time dilation of clocks and rulers his eyes are following.)
Accelerating
- [[Rindler Coordinates]]   (Coordinates for "Born Rigid" accelerating observers -- Maintains proper length.)
- [[Bell Coordinates]] (Coordinates for Bell's Spaceship accelerating observers -- Stretched string between rockets)
- [[Newtonian Coordinates]] Acceleration follows $x=1/2 a t^2$ hence getting faster than light after some time.

Initial Distribution
- [[Milne Coordinates]]  Each of an infinite number of particlse emanating from a single event in time in space has an initial rapidity.
