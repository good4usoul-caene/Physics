| [Dots on a Moving Cone](https://www.desmos.com/3d/yszbdnhrii)                         | A 2D + Time animation of several moving dots (tilted world-lines) as they are "seen" from the origin.  The dots are seen when and where the world-lines cross the past-light-cone of the observer.  More specifically, they are seen *from* the origin [[here and now]] where the observer is seeing them.  The image locations are the $(x,y)$ coordinates of the world-line/light-cone intersection.  The *time* of the image is not necessarily relevant to the observer. |
| [fencerow](https://www.desmos.com/calculator/9llnokw0mf)                              | This is a nonrelativistic view of a bunch of circles passing by along a fence-row.  This is essentially what people would expect to see if an object passed by along an infinite, straight, fencerow at high but non-relativistic speeds.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| [Relativistic-Image-of-Passing-Circles](https://www.desmos.com/calculator/qax22pgbo1) | This is a similar demonstration, except that all relativistic effects have been added.  In particular, the oncoming object appears stretched out and quite fast.  After it passes, the object appears contracted and slower.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
