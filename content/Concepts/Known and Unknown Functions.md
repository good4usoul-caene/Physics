
### Compare to
 - [[Local and Global Mapping]]

Consider the position of a vertically thrown ball in gravitational field $g$ described by
$$y(t)=y_0+v_{y0} t - \frac{1}{2}g t^2$$
With $y_0$ represents the height of the ball, and $v_{y0}$ being the vertical velocity of the ball we can say a great deal about the ball's position, but there are things outside the domain.

Outside the domain:
 - Before the ball was thrown
 - After the ball is caught or hits the ground.

In a great many cases, the domain of applicability of the [[Zoom-In Principle]] and the domain of where a function is known is the same.
