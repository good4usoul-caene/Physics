# Metric Isoclines in Polar and Rectangular Coordinates
 Consider the [[Polar to Rectangular Transformation]]
 
The transformation of interest converts from ==polar coordinates== to ==rectangular coordinates== .

With this in mind, we can describe four metric isoclines

1. Horizontal lines:  $$\Align{y&=\text{constant}\\\Rightarrow  dy &= 0}$$
2. Vertical Lines:  $$\Align{x&=\text{constant}\\\Rightarrow dx&=0}$$
3. Circles around the origin:  $$\Align{r&=\text{constant}\\\Rightarrow  dr &= 0}$$
4. Rays pointing from the center: $$\Align{\phi&=\text{constant}\\\Rightarrow  d\phi &= 0}$$
