A perspectival transformation is one that imagines "what would the situation look like from that perspective"?  

The main perspectival transformations are 
 - Rotation: (imagines the perspective of someone who has turned or turned around)
 - Galilean transformation (imagines the perspective of someone traveling at a different velocity)
 - [[Lorentz Transformations]] (imagines the perspective of someone traveling at a different velocity)

While both the Galilean and Lorentz Transformations are described the same way, here, they are different models and expectation of what the different perspective ought look like.

Descriptive transformations, on the other hand may
 - [[Polar to Rectangular Transformation]]: (converts cartesian coordinates to angle and radius)
 - [[Spherical to Rectangular Coordinates]] (converts Cartesian coordinates to yaw (polar), pitch (azimuthal) and distance)

Noninertial transformations could be in part perspectival and in part descriptive.  
  - Rindler Coordinates: May imagine the view of an observer on a Born Rigid accelerating rocket.  Or the view of an "instantaneous boost:  See [[Lorentz Transformations]]"
  - Rotating coordinates: Generally imagines the view of a person standing at the center of a rotating disk, keeping his eyes on a point on the rotating disk, OR imagines a distant object moving along a straight line as he turns his head to follow it.
