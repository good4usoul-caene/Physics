### Matrix Forms

These boosts take the velocity $v_\eta=c\tanh\eta$ and render it stationary ($v_\eta'=0)$, and take the velocity $v_0=0$ and send it to $v_0'=c\tanh(-\eta)$.  If the domain is all of space and time, it means we are changing our perspective to that of an avatar (See [[1st and 3rd Person]]) moving to the right with velocity $v_\eta$.  If the domain is the avatar itself, we should use the inverse transformation.
