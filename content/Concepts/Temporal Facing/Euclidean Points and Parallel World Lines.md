>Euclid defined
>
>>A point is that which has no part.

By this, he meant that a point has no spatial extent in any direction.  It has 
 - no width, 
 - no height, 
 - no breadth

However, if his analysis were updated with regards to Special Relativity, **two properties** actually ought to be added to the definition

Euclid's points also had:
-  **Temporal Extent**: Euclid's points never vanished in the instant their position was determined
 - **A Stationary Property:** Euclid's geometry is built on points, lines, and arcs drawn on a static medium.  They never moved around the page.
