Astronomers usually use "Right Ascension" and "Declination" to determine the location of stars in the sky.  In this coordinate system the constellations of the zodiac climb in declination to about 23.44 degrees on one side, and descend to about 23.44 degrees on the other side.

The constellations of the zodiac, though are on the **ecliptic plane** rather than on earth's equatorial plane.  This is the plane along which earth orbits the sun.  The other 7 planets of the solar system are aligned to this same plane with only negligible differences.
