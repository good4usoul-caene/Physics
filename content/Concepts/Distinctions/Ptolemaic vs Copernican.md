# The Breakthrough
The Copernican revolution is often cited as a great break-through because the Ptolemaic (earth centered) geometry was disbanded for the Copernican (sun centered) geometry.  

It is often cited as though the hypotheses were tested against the scientific method, and the Copernican method was found to make predictions that the Ptolemaic system could not.

Hence the scientific method had essentially killed the Ptolemaic geometry.

# Ptolemy isn't dead

However, in truth, Ptolemaic geometry is not dead.  The Copernican model superior for making predictions because it was simpler.  But this does not actually invalidate the earth centered [[Point-of-View]].  

Quite the contrary, the earth-centered geometry is alive and well in the form of the Earth-Centered Coordinate [[Abbreviations|(ECI)]] system, used to synchronize clocks to Coordinated Universal Time  [[Abbreviations|(UTC)]].
 
# Simplest Solution

The simplest solution to predicting the paths of planets around the solar system is to convert our observations to a sun-centered coordinate system, run our modeling there, then convert back to our earth-centered, and ground-centered coordinates to predict what we will actually see from earth's surface.  

The ground-centered and earth-centered coordinates are not invalid.  While the scientific method has been quite successful in solving many problems, invalidating the Ptolemaic coordinate system is **not** one of those successes.  Instead, much scientific progress has been made to make the Ptolemaic coordinate system much more predictable.
