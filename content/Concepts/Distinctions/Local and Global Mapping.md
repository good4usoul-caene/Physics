The [[Lorentz Transformations]] are a Global Mapping.  They take all of the events in space and time as the Domain and map them to all events in space and time as the Range.  It might be worth applying a [[Zoom-Out Principle]] describing the perspectival changes of Lorentz Transformations to compare to the [[Zoom-In Principle]] applied in modeling physical changes due to laws of mechanics.

On the other hand, the [[metric tensor]] $g_{\mu\nu}$ is a local mapping, taking, for example a tiny interval, $(d\tau, dr, d\phi)$  and mapping it to $(dt,dx,dy)$ in such a way that either the [[Minkowski Metric]] is conserved, or that a [[Lagrangian, Action, and Euler-Lagrange Equations]] is conserved.
