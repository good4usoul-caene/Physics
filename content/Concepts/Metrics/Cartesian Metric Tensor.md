Since we have something called the "[[Minkowski Metric]] Tensor", which is the diagonal matrix $\operatorname{Diag}(-1,1,1,1)$ it would seem reasonable to have something simpler to compare it to, before we move on to more complex things like the "Polar Metric Tensor" or the "Spherical Metric Tensor".  

So, I introduce, the Cartesian Metric Tensor, which is the Identity matrix; $$I=\operatorname{Diag}(1,1,1)$$
The Cartesian Metric Tensor is the characteristic of the length of a differential path element in Cartesian coordinates

$$dh^2=dx^2+dy^2+dz^2\\
=\overset\rightarrow {d(x,y,z)} (I) d(x,y,z)\downarrow
\\=\begin{pmatrix}dx&dy&dz\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\0&1&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}dx\\dy\\dz\end{pmatrix}
$$
