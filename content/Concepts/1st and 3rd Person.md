There are two main ways of thinking of [[Hyperbolic Trig Identites|Rotation]] and [[Boost]].  

In video games an "avatar" is frequently introduced, using definition 2 of the following choices:

**Avatar**
 1. a manifestation of a deity or released soul in bodily form on earth; an incarnate divine teacher.
 2. an icon or figure representing a particular person in a video game, internet forum, etc.:

Special Relativity needs to expand this definition, or find another word to represent the figure that actually *is* a particular person in the real universe.

3. A person or drone occupying a [[Point-of-View]]:  In the real universe, there is some sense by which we are each avatars carrying our own consciousness.

  We can use our muscles to influence what our avatar does:  I can, for instance, induce my avatar to rotate or accelerate by using my muscles.  When I turn right, the universe around me appears to turn left.  Hence a rotation transformation might have two different domains, depending on the context.

If I turn left, everyone sees this rotation and says "his avatar turned left".  The domain of the rotation operation was all of the "me"-particles.  Nothing else in the universe was influenced.

However, from my perspective it is exactly the opposite.  The only particles that weren't affected by my rotation were the "me"-particles.  Every other point in the universe was influenced by my rotation.  

Likewise, when I accelerate, everyone else in the universe only sees my particles undergo acceleration, but from my [[Point-of-View]] every particle in the universe has changed velocity.
