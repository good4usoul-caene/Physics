
### Principle
Don't be tempted to defend a position that you do not hold.  

### What Is a Strawman
A "Strawman argument" is a claim that no one is actually making.  There are two ways that a strawman argument may appear in a debate.
 1. A failed [[Heuristic Hypotheses|Heuristic Hypothesis]]:  Someone may simply be misunderstanding your position
 2. Dishonesty:  Someone may know that your position is correct, but if your language is not clear, they may be able to fool an audience into thinking that you're making a different claim altogether.

### Reaction to Strawman
The best method to deal with a strawman is to disavow it entirely, clarify your own position and why it differs from the strawman argument.  If the Strawman is due to a failed Heuristic Hypothesis, it should be possible to re-direct the conversation toward something more productive.

### Reaction to Repeated Strawman
After a time, if someone continues to [[Principles/Principles of Debate/bear false witness]] against you or the ideas that you are defending, the best you can do is make a note of it, and if possible, ignore them.
