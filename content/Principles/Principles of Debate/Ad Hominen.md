
Ad Hominin, or "to the man" arguments focus on attacking a person's character or ability, rather than acknowledging their ides.  

Unfortunately, for some, it is a common tactic in a debate over ideas to make personal attacks.  There is no real limit on such behavior.   

# Tactics of people who like Ad Hominin arguments:
1. Virtually any concession or explanation can be turned into a moral failure:  "Oh, you wanted to be "x" when you grew up?  I don't see why you're whining here about it."
2. Any question or clarification can be interpreted as an ad hominin attack.  "You are trying to make it look like I don't know that!"
3. When evidence is unavailable, moral failures can be fabricated.

# Responding to Ad Hominin Arguments
1. In a forum situation, unless you want to derail the conversation you're having with others, it is probably best to simply ignore a person who interprets every interaction as a personal attack.
