A common "explanation" of Special Relativity is to invoke "[[Spacetime]]" by saying "We now understand that space and time are the same."  However [[Avoid Conflation|Conflation]] of two easily distinguished ideas does not actually have much in the way of explanatory power.  

Distances still have units of distance.  Times have units of time.  

The revelation that ==we can convert time into space== is not novel or new--it is called "==movement==".  By changing your velocity $\Delta v$ and then waiting $\Delta t$, one arrives at another location.

What is novel and new with regards to Special Relativity is the precise mechanism ([[Lorentz Transformations]]) by which someone changes their velocity and waits.
