It seems a common tactic in online debates to "feint" as defined by "appearing to attack from one direction" while actually attacking from another direction.

In Special Relativity questions, the feint usually comes in the form of a badly defined [[thought experiment]].  

Some problems that make it badly formed are:
- Not enough information (easily fixed by adding necessary information)
- Requiring the answer to meet conditions the idea explicitly rejects.
	- Common example:  Requiring that absolute simultaneity be obtained by Special Relativity
- Modifying the question as the argument moves along, so that every answer given can be said to be incorrect.

Each of these tactics could be considered a "feint", because they are attempts to trick the [[Pin Yourself Down|idea's defender]] to waste time in an argument where the "attacker's" goal is not to learn, but to defeat.
