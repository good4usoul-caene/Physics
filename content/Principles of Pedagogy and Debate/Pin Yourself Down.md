In a debate of ideas, one should desire to play defense more than offense.  

But the defense of an idea is not accomplished by "dodging".  Dodging is an excellent defense for someone who is in a physical fight.  Dodging is not being where your opponent expects you to be.  However, in an argument over ideas, dodging means that you do not have any real position to defend. 

A good defense means that 
 - You have an idea to defend
 - You understand the idea well enough to defend it.
 - You are willing to explore various questions applicable to the idea.
