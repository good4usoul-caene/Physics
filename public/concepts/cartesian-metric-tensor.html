Cartesian Metric Tensor

Oct 05, 20261 min read

Since we have something called the “Minkowski Metric Tensor”, which is the diagonal matrix it would seem reasonable to have something simpler to compare it to, before we move on to more complex things like the “Polar Metric Tensor” or the “Spherical Metric Tensor”.

So, I introduce, the Cartesian Metric Tensor, which is the Identity matrix;
The Cartesian Metric Tensor is the characteristic of the length of a differential path element in Cartesian coordinates
